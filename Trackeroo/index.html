What is Trackeroo?

Trackeroo is a lightweight, secure, and user-friendly mobile app that lets you track live locations in real-time. Whether you're coordinating with friends, keeping an eye on your kids, or running a delivery business — Trackeroo ensures you always know where everyone is.

Why Trackeroo?

Because real-time matters. Trackeroo is built with modern technology that delivers precise GPS tracking with minimal battery usage.

Use Cases

Trackeroo is designed to simplify location tracking for a wide range of everyday scenarios. Parents can use it to ensure their children arrive safely at school or home. Delivery and logistics teams can monitor fleet movement in real-time to improve efficiency. Friends and family can stay connected during trips or outings. Event organizers can manage crowd flow, while businesses can track field agents with ease. Whether it’s for personal safety, coordination, or productivity — Trackeroo adapts to your needs.
